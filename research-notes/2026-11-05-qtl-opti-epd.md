## What the sourced announcement supports

- Inside Lighting published QTL’s industry announcement on November 3, 2026. It says QTL had published an independently verified Environmental Product Declaration (EPD) for its OPTI aluminium linear LED fixture family, and identifies OPTI as QTL’s seventh fixture family with a product-specific EPD. [Source article](https://inside.lighting/press/26-q4/qtl-publishes-verified-carbon-footprint-opti-linear-led-fixtures-its-seventh-family-product-specific-epd)
- The announcement identifies the EPD as registration **QTRN-00006-V01.01-EN**, verified through the PEP ecopassport programme to ISO 14025, using QTL’s U.S. manufacturing data. Its stated functional unit is 1,000 lumens over 35,000 hours; it reports total life-cycle global-warming potential of **309 kg CO2e**, with about **97%** attributed to electricity during operation. [Source article](https://inside.lighting/press/26-q4/qtl-publishes-verified-carbon-footprint-opti-linear-led-fixtures-its-seventh-family-product-specific-epd)
- QTL’s own OPTI brochure independently identifies the family as an aluminium linear fixture with surface-mounted or recessed installation options and multiple Lambertian and optical-lens options. [QTL OPTI brochure](https://www.qtl.lighting/Customer-Content/www/brochures/Files/OPTI_BiFold.pdf?d=110001120000)

## Editorial limits for the FLOSEEK item

This is a manufacturer-supplied industry announcement, not an independent comparison of fixtures or a completed-project study. Preserve the source’s boundary: the stated carbon figure is tied to the identified EPD functional unit, data source and life-cycle assumptions. Do not infer whole-building carbon, operating energy, LEED credits, cost, optical performance, controls compatibility, warranty, availability, indoor-air performance, acoustic performance or a project outcome.

For acoustic-lighting buyers, frame the relevance as a documentation and coordination prompt. An EPD can support a responsible product comparison, but a ceiling proposal still needs the exact luminaire configuration, photometry, glare, controls, mounting and service details alongside independently appropriate acoustic evidence and room-level design verification.
