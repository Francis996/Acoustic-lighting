## Suggested factual outline for the entry

SATCO’s October 15 launch introduces Touch-Select Slimfit Downlights, a Plus Series offering with a flat lens and remote driver. The company says the lens is used to select colour temperature, and lists 4-inch and 6-inch sizes, a 110-degree beam angle and a slimfit design that does not require a housing. It suggests the range for multifamily, residential, education and hospitality projects.

SATCO’s S12910 listing documents one 4-inch configuration at 9 W, 750 lumens and 90 CRI, with five selectable CCT options from 2700 K to 5000 K. That listing also gives 50,000 rated hours, phase-cut dimming, wet-location and IC ratings, and a five-year warranty. These are manufacturer-stated product details, not completed-space measurements or a substitute for a lighting calculation.

For FLOSEEK buyers, the update is a prompt to coordinate recessed lighting with the ceiling and acoustic package early. Confirm which spaces genuinely need recessed light, fixture and driver access, clearances and penetrations, acoustical ceiling construction and coverage, HVAC/AV/life-safety interfaces, field CCT policy, controls, photometry and glare review. Where acoustic treatment is required, this downlight should be assessed as a lighting component within a separate room-acoustic strategy rather than as an acoustic solution.
