## What the source supports

- LightArt's official product post, dated October 26, 2026, introduces Radielle as a compact pendant collection for commercial interior touchpoints including restaurant tables, hotel guestroom desks, conference breakout tables and reception areas. [LightArt announcement](https://lightart.com/blog/post/small-light-serious-work-introducing-radielle)
- The launch includes sphere, teardrop, cone and pill pendants in small and large sizes, plus a Candlestick system in two diameters and multiple lengths. The manufacturer says fixtures can be specified singly, in repeated runs or in grouped ceiling compositions. [LightArt announcement](https://lightart.com/blog/post/small-light-serious-work-introducing-radielle)
- According to LightArt, Radielle offers 15 standard colours, in-house 3D-printed shades using 90% molecularly recycled PETG, 30°, 54° or 78° beam distributions, 2700K–4000K CCTs, 80 or 90 CRI, warm dimming, and 0–10V, TRIAC and ELV dimming; PoE and an integrated emergency-battery option are also listed. [LightArt announcement](https://lightart.com/blog/post/small-light-serious-work-introducing-radielle)
- LightArt lists adjustable suspension, PVC-free black or white cord, a TGIC-free powder-coated canopy, a five-year warranty on standard components, and dry/damp interior rating. [LightArt announcement](https://lightart.com/blog/post/small-light-serious-work-introducing-radielle)
