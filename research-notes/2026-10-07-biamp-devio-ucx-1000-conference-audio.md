## Editorial guardrails

- This is a first-party manufacturer press release. Attribute certification, component features, compatibility, audio outcomes and commissioning claims to Biamp; it is not an independent performance test or a completed workplace case study.
- The release gives no room dimensions, baseline or post-commissioning acoustic measurements, reverberation time, speech-transmission result, absorption treatment, lighting specification, glare metric, sound-pressure level or pricing. Do not claim that the bundle corrects poor room acoustics or replaces acoustic treatment.
- The defensible B2B angle is coordination for hybrid rooms: technology can automate endpoint calibration, but ceiling/wall absorption, speech privacy, HVAC noise, microphone coverage, lighting location and control scenes still need to be designed together for the specific room.
- For acoustic-lighting buyers, treat the announcement as relevant adjacent infrastructure. Specify acoustic luminaires on their own tested absorption configuration and coordinate their suspension, photometry, glare, illuminance, controls, access and maintenance with the conferencing bar and microphones.
