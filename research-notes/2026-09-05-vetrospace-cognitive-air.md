## Verified facts suitable for copy

1. Vetrospace says it is introducing its Cognitive Air™ system across all office pods and phone booths, with patent-pending Ambient Vectoring™ technology. [Vetrospace’s announcement](https://vetrospace.com/stories/vetrospaces-innovation-tackles-the-invisible-problem-in-acoustic-office-pods)
2. The company describes the intended problem as stale air, rising carbon dioxide and heat build-up in soundproof workspaces. [Vetrospace’s announcement](https://vetrospace.com/stories/vetrospaces-innovation-tackles-the-invisible-problem-in-acoustic-office-pods)
3. Vetrospace says Adaptive CO₂ Control monitors conditions inside and outside the pod and adjusts ventilation according to measured load. [Vetrospace’s announcement](https://vetrospace.com/stories/vetrospaces-innovation-tackles-the-invisible-problem-in-acoustic-office-pods)
4. The company describes Ambient Vectoring™ as directing exhaust air to mix with surrounding air or towards a building extract system. [Vetrospace’s announcement](https://vetrospace.com/stories/vetrospaces-innovation-tackles-the-invisible-problem-in-acoustic-office-pods)
