## Confirmed facts used in the news entry

1. Zilenzio’s release was published September 24, 2026 and announces Zonify, created with Studio Stockholm, for digital meetings and large wall surfaces. [Launch release](https://www.mynewsdesk.com/se/zilenzio/pressreleases/zilenzio-and-studio-stockholm-launch-zonify-for-better-digital-meetings-and-large-wall-surfaces-3468670)
2. The release describes Zonify Table as a desk-attached screen that combines pleated textile and sound-absorbing stone wool with dimmable indirect lighting, a wireless remote and standard wall-outlet connection. It lists a 1,200 × 860 mm configuration with NRC 0.70, SAA 0.68 and alpha-w 0.65. [Launch release](https://www.mynewsdesk.com/se/zilenzio/pressreleases/zilenzio-and-studio-stockholm-launch-zonify-for-better-digital-meetings-and-large-wall-surfaces-3468670)
3. The release describes Zonify Wall as pleated textile on a roll, installed between top and bottom rails, with optional stone wool fixed behind it. It lists 800 mm width and standard 1,000, 1,500, 2,000 and 2,500 mm lengths. [Launch release](https://www.mynewsdesk.com/se/zilenzio/pressreleases/zilenzio-and-studio-stockholm-launch-zonify-for-better-digital-meetings-and-large-wall-surfaces-3468670)
4. Zilenzio’s product page identifies stone wool, fabric and solid-wood cleats; it reports the wall configuration with stone wool as NRC 0.75, SAA 0.75 and alpha-w 0.65, tested to SS-EN ISO 354:2003 and ISO 20189:2018. It also provides separate lower values for the version without stone wool. [Zonify Wall product page](https://zilenzio.com/product/zonify-wall/)

## Editorial guardrails

- Treat the release as manufacturer product information, not independent evidence of a completed office, video-call outcome, acoustic calculation, lighting calculation, standard compliance or field study.
- Keep acoustic figures tied to the named product configuration, material and test context. Do not transfer them into room-level reverberation, speech intelligibility, speech privacy, noise isolation or productivity claims.
- Do not claim illuminance, glare, camera performance, energy use, installation duration, pricing or availability. Those are not published in the source.
- A supportable B2B interpretation is to coordinate the selected acoustic-lighting or acoustic-screen configuration with activities, surfaces, HVAC and AV noise, lighting criteria, power, controls, mounting, access and project-specific calculation or commissioning.
