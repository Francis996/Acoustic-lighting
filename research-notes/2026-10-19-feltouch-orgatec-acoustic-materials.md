## Facts supported by the source

- Feltouch says it will show more than 25 products and developments at ORGATEC 2026 in Cologne, October 27–30.
- The company says its Zero Burn programme began in 2020 and reintegrates approximately 140 tonnes of felt-production waste into production annually; Zero Brick is described as being made from its own waste without binders.
- Feltouch says it will introduce a biodegradable acoustic panel, printed felt surfaces with wood, corten, travertine and stone-inspired appearances, three new Mandarin colours, and digital product-selection tools.
- The company describes its RT60 Calculator as an early-stage tool for calculating reverberation time and simulating potential Feltouch-solution performance.
