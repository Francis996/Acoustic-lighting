## Editorial guardrails

- This is a manufacturer’s launch announcement, not an independent evaluation or a room-acoustic study. Attribute product, availability and operating-workflow claims to Duni.
- The release does not state product-level photometry, glare, ingress rating, battery life, charging duration, electrical approvals, pricing, country-by-country availability, acoustic properties or installed-project outcomes. Obtain the selected product’s current technical documents before specification.
- The supportable B2B angle is operational coordination: hospitality teams can assess controls, scene governance, charging, cleaning and replacement workflow together with desired ambience. It is not evidence that cordless fixtures alone improve guest comfort or an interior’s acoustic performance.
- For acoustic-lighting projects, integrate table lighting with the ceiling-and-wall acoustic scheme rather than treating it as a substitute. Confirm task and ambient light levels, controls, glare, maintenance routes, room finish, noise sources and tested acoustic treatment at project level.
