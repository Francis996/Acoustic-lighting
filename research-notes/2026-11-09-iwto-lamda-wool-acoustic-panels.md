# IWTO 2026 Innovation in Wool Awards — LAMDA acoustic panels

**Recommended item:** *Wull Technologies’ LAMDA Wins 2026 Innovation in Wool Award for Binder-Free Acoustic Panels*  
**Publisher:** International Wool Textile Organisation (IWTO)  
**Published:** November 5, 2026  
**Canonical source:** <https://iwto.org/innovation-in-wool-awards-2026/>

## What the source supports

- IWTO published “British Wool Takes New Shape: 2026 Innovation in Wool Awards Winners” on November 5, 2026. The article says the awards were announced by the Campaign for Wool and that Wull Technologies’ LAMDA received the main award.
- IWTO describes LAMDA as a rigid, self-supporting material made from 100% British sheep wool, with no added plastics, resins, starches or latex. It says the material itself forms the structure.
- The article identifies LAMDA Hush acoustic panels and baffles as LAMDA’s first commercial application and says they are installed in Manchester’s Sister Building. It also says one cubic metre of material uses roughly 140 kg of clean wool.
- The source says packaging and insulation applications could follow, subject to further testing and accreditation. It identifies the Innovation in Wool Awards as established in 2018, with judging support from Campaign for Wool and Dame Mary Archer leading the 2026 judging panel.

## Supporting, independent context

- Innovate UK Business Connect identifies LAMDA as a patent-pending rigid material with no plastic binders, synthetic fibres or added resins. It says LAMDA Hush acoustic panels and baffles have been installed in a Manchester building and that the product targets commercial interiors and construction: <https://iuk-business-connect.org.uk/projects/women-in-innovation/ruth-marie-mackrodt/>.

## Editorial limits

IWTO’s article is an award report from a wool-industry organisation, not an independent acoustic test report, product declaration, fire test, project case study or whole-life assessment. It does not publish absorption coefficients, test standard, mounting condition, room data, fire performance, VOC content, durability, cleaning, dimensions, price, lead time, warranty, lighting photometry or a measurement of the Sister Building installation.

Do not infer acoustic performance or sustainability outcomes from the award, the binder-free description or the installation alone. For a project, request current product-specific acoustic and fire documentation, the tested construction and mounting condition, required coverage, support and ceiling details, lighting/services coordination, maintenance information and room-level acceptance criteria.
