## What the sources support

- RGB Communications published its article on November 2, 2026. It describes the XSCACE Ghost as an ultra-slim architectural speaker for residential and commercial interiors, intended to sit nearly flush with walls and ceilings; its stated surface profile is 0.3 mm.
- The distributor frames the product as a response to visible bezels, mounting rings and recessed grilles that can disrupt minimalist interior schemes. That is an aesthetic and integration claim, not a room-acoustic result.
- The manufacturer’s current product page identifies Ghost 2.0 as an 80 W, 92 dB, 4-ohm slim in-ceiling speaker with a stated 80–20,000 Hz range. It also says its Floorplan Sound Simulation can predict coverage, room modes and reflections, while XSCACE Studio offers on-site tools including RT60, SPL, delay/level/polarity, frequency-response and THD measurement.

## Editorial limits

RGB is XSCACE’s UK distributor and the article is promotional product news, not an independent test report or project case study. Neither source provides the Ghost’s acoustic absorption, reverberation-control or speech-privacy data; it is an audio/loudspeaker product, not an acoustic treatment.

Do not treat nominal speaker specifications as a system design, or claim a particular room’s coverage, intelligibility, sound level, uniformity, sound isolation or acoustic-lighting performance. Require the applicable cut sheet, enclosure/back-box and ceiling details, amplifier/DSP design, loudspeaker-layout calculation, fire and code review, access, commissioning scope and measured acceptance criteria for the project.
