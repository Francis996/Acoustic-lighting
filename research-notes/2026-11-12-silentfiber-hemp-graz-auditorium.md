## What the news source reports

- MaterialDistrict's November 12 report documents the refurbishment of the main auditorium at Graz University of Technology with SilentFiber's SilentHEMP panels. It says panels in grey, charcoal and sand were installed over the timber walls to reduce reverberation and support speech intelligibility.
- The report identifies the product as 62.5 × 62.5 × 4.5 cm, and credits Synthesa's in-house colour-design studio with the auditorium colour concept. It also reports an EN 15804+A2 EPD result of –2.23 kg CO2e/m² for modules A1–A3, explicitly limited to production and not the use or end-of-life stages.

## Primary-source corroboration

- SilentFiber's own product page lists the hemp panel dimensions, wall/ceiling use, alpha-w 0.75 sound absorption rating, eight colours, and its stated composition (84% hemp straw, 15% bicomponent binding fibre and less than 1% fire-retardant salt): <https://www.silentfiber.net/en/product/hanf-akustikplatte/>.
- The 2026 International Excellence Awards entry, submitted by project participant Synthesa, identifies the project as the Auditorium at Graz University of Technology and independently supports the project use, alpha-w 0.75 value and –2.23 kg CO2e/m² A1–A3 EPD figure: <https://designskill.org/winner/silenthemp-the-co%E2%82%82-positive-acoustic-panel-gold-winner-2026-sustainable-and-circular-product-design-ie-awards/>.

## Editorial limits

This is an editorial project report, supported by a manufacturer product page and award-project entry; it is not a commissioned acoustic measurement report for the completed auditorium.
