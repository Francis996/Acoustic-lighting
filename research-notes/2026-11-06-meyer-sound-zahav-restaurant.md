## What the source supports

- Meyer Sound's November 4, 2026 case study says Philadelphia restaurant Zahav installed a Constellation active-acoustics system powered by the NADIA digital audio platform, combined with acoustic treatment that includes a Libra panel.
- The article identifies four acoustic zones in the compact dining room: the bar, two central seating areas and a kitchen-facing section. It names 16 microphones, 13 UP-4slim loudspeakers, seven MM-10XP subwoofers and two USW-112P subwoofers in the main room, plus Ashby-8C and UP-4slim loudspeakers in two smaller dining areas.
- The source identifies ceiling fans, HVAC, decorative canopies, limited ceiling access and daily dinner service as installation-coordination constraints. It frames the installation as an example of the Constellation Focus application for localized clarity and natural sound masking.

## Editorial limits

This is a Meyer Sound supplier case study. It does not publish independent before/after acoustic measurements, reverberation time, STI, SPL limits, coverage model, latency, tuning, baseline room condition, lighting design, cost, maintenance scope or a passive-only comparison. Do not infer those results for another hospitality project.

For acoustic-lighting buyers, use the case as a coordination signal: set room-level acoustic and lighting targets, validate passive absorber coverage, then coordinate any active system with luminaires, ceiling services, controls, access and commissioning rather than treating it as a substitute for architectural acoustic design.
