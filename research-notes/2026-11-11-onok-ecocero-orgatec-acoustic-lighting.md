## What the source supports

- Onok Lighting published its Senses-at-ORGATEC account on November 11, 2026. It says ECOcero exhibited Senses in Cologne from October 27 to 30, where the stand combined acoustics, material and light; Onok supplied the lighting.
- The manufacturer says ECOcero's PET-fibre panels use recycled bottles. Its stand included a canopy of overlapping acoustic pieces, arches, curved shelving and relief panels, with Noé Prades named as designer.
- Onok says concealed COBPlus 1006 LED neon followed the organic panel contours, washing the relief from the bottom up; MultiFlex 1010 3D neon was used for tighter radii. It identifies both as 24 V, IP67 and CRI above 90, with Casambi control.
- The article states that lighting and panel layout were jointly defined from the beginning, including factory-built recesses/channels; it also describes full-scale material tests and made-to-measure prepared LED runs.
