## Confirmed facts for the news entry

1. Signify published the release on September 9, 2026, about a lighting upgrade at the London Aquatics Centre. [Original release](https://www.signify.com/en-gb/our-company/news/press-releases/2026/20260909-london-aquatics-centre)
2. The release says operator Everyone Active replaced the venue’s legacy HID lighting and that a project requirement was the World Aquatics television-broadcast standard of **1,500 lux Ev**. [Original release](https://www.signify.com/en-gb/our-company/news/press-releases/2026/20260909-london-aquatics-centre)
3. According to Signify, the installation comprises **168 ArenaVision LED gen3.5 luminaires** for competition lighting and **16 UniFlood Flat luminaires** for added flexibility, paired with a DMX control system. [Original release](https://www.signify.com/en-gb/our-company/news/press-releases/2026/20260909-london-aquatics-centre)
4. Signify says an augmented-reality aiming workflow used a pool-area grid and a smartphone attached to each luminaire to align fixtures suspended as much as 40 metres above the water. It presents the approach as a way to reduce time working at height and match the lighting design intent. [Original release](https://www.signify.com/en-gb/our-company/news/press-releases/2026/20260909-london-aquatics-centre)
5. Signify reports that, at full capacity, the LED solution delivers **more than 60% energy savings** compared with the prior HID lighting. This is a supplier-reported, venue-specific comparison; the release does not state the measurement period, load profile, metering method, baseline configuration or whole-site energy effect. [Original release](https://www.signify.com/en-gb/our-company/news/press-releases/2026/20260909-london-aquatics-centre)

## Editorial guardrails

- This is a **Signify press release** about one sports-venue project. Attribute installed quantities, the World Aquatics criterion, energy-savings comparison, aiming workflow, visibility, broadcast compliance and operational outcomes to Signify or the named project participants; it is not independent product testing.
- Use “more than 60% energy savings at full capacity compared with the previous HID lighting,” not a rounded or unconditional saving claim. Do not extrapolate it to another building, operating schedule, dimming profile, sports venue, office, acoustic luminaire, site or whole-facility bill.
- The release does not provide product photometry, glare assessment, power data, controls programming, maintenance interval, lighting calculation, commissioning records, measurements, warranty, pricing, room finishes, acoustic design, reverberation result, speech/privacy outcome or acoustic test data. Those require the selected configuration and project-specific evidence.
- The useful B2B takeaway is coordination: for hard-to-access fixtures, evaluate the lighting calculation and aiming/commissioning method alongside access strategy, suspension and electrical interfaces, controls, cleaning and replacement routes, reflectance/glare, safety, programme and acceptance testing. This is an inference from the project description, not a claim from a general standard.
- Do not claim an acoustic benefit or imply that broadcast-sports lighting performance transfers to commercial interiors or acoustic lighting. Where acoustic luminaires are considered, model the room separately and request configuration- and mounting-specific absorption data in addition to photometry, glare, controls, electrical, structural, fire/compliance and service evidence.
