## Facts supported by the source

- Live Nation says transparent Unplugs earplugs are available to buy at 60 Live Nation venues in the United States through a partnership that includes a Live Nation equity investment.
- The company says a two-mode mechanical switch changes how much sound passes through without requiring batteries or an app. It describes the closed setting as using acoustic filters to reduce volume while retaining clarity, and the open setting as allowing more sound through for conversation.
- Live Nation says complimentary foam earplugs will continue to be available at participating venues, alongside the Unplugs product.
- The release says Unplugs are sold through vending machines, sound guides, and merchandise and guest-services stands. It also says the product will be supplied to venue employees and made available to selected artists and touring teams through green-room gifting.
- The source reports that three in four fans say hearing protection should be an essential part of a concert experience and that nearly 60% of fans who have used earplugs at live-music events have removed them to talk. The release does not disclose its research method, sample, dates or margin of error for either figure.
