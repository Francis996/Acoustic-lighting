## Confirmed facts for the news entry

1. Ecophon says it will attend Skånsk Byggtjänst's *Våtrum och Interiör* event in Luleå, Skellefteå and Umeå from September 29 to October 1, 2026. The official item gives one location and time for each city.
2. The company invites attendees to discuss improving sound environments where hygiene and cleaning requirements are high. It explicitly names healthcare, laboratories, clean rooms and other hygiene-demanding environments.
3. Ecophon frames the relevant design brief as combining good sound absorption with functional, durable solutions. It also says the event can cover colours, design and reuse, and how acoustics can become a natural part of a project's whole.
