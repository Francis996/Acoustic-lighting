## Editorial guardrails

- This is a **company press release** about a third-party sustainability-management rating. Attribute factual claims about Signify’s result and programmes to Signify and do not portray the rating as an independent test of a specified luminaire, lighting system or installed commercial project.
- Do not claim that a Platinum medal, company-level Environment score, LED, connected system, Signify Circle program, repair/reuse/take-back/recycling activity or this article itself guarantees a product’s energy use, embodied carbon, circularity, recycled content, EPD, warranty, acoustic performance, indoor-environment outcome, building certification credit or project compliance.
- The source does not specify products, configurations, geographic availability, material composition, service routes, take-back eligibility, lifecycle-assessment boundary, lighting calculation, photometry, controls configuration, price, lead time, acoustic treatment, or an installed-project result. These require selected-product and project-specific evidence.
- The publishable B2B takeaway is procurement process, not product performance: ask a lighting supplier for current, scope-specific evidence that maps its stated sustainability commitments to the nominated products, markets, installation, maintenance, end-of-life route and the project’s own reporting requirements.
- Acoustic lighting can be mentioned only as a category of lighting product needing the same configuration-specific evidence plus acoustic-test data, photometry/glare, electrical/control, suspension, fire/compliance, maintenance and room-level coordination. This release contains no acoustic-lighting evidence.
