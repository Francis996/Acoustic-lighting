## Confirmed facts for a news entry

1. Hushoffice announced new exterior finishes and interior-fabric options for both its Hushfree and Hushclassic acoustic-pod ranges on **October 7, 2026**. [Original Hushoffice release](https://hushoffice.prowly.com/473632-hushoffice-introduces-new-colour-finishes-for-its-acoustic-pods)
2. The company describes the release as a coordinated workplace-design system, intended to support focused work, collaboration and a range of user needs rather than acting as disconnected colour choices. [Original Hushoffice release](https://hushoffice.prowly.com/473632-hushoffice-introduces-new-colour-finishes-for-its-acoustic-pods)
3. Hushoffice says Hushfree receives five exterior colours, two additional tabletop finishes, five Tonal fabric finishes and a carpet option; Hushclassic receives five exterior colours, 12 Camira RePlay fabric finishes, three tabletop finishes, five edge colours and a carpet finish. [Original Hushoffice release](https://hushoffice.prowly.com/473632-hushoffice-introduces-new-colour-finishes-for-its-acoustic-pods)
4. The named enclosure finishes are Clay Red, Stream Blue, Slate Green, Light Oak and Taupe. Hushoffice says it will show the colour finishes at Orgatec in Cologne, October 27–30, 2026. [Original Hushoffice release](https://hushoffice.prowly.com/473632-hushoffice-introduces-new-colour-finishes-for-its-acoustic-pods)
