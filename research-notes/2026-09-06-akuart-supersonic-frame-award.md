## Verified facts suitable for copy

1. AKUART says its Supersonic Wall Panel received Gold at the FRAME Awards, recognising a combination of acoustic performance, architectural design and circular thinking. [AKUART’s announcement](https://akuart.com/supersonic-wall-panel-gold-at-the-frame-awards/)
2. AKUART reports that an international jury of architects, designers and creative leaders evaluated the system, where it scored particularly strongly across functionality, form and eco-social impact. [AKUART’s announcement](https://akuart.com/supersonic-wall-panel-gold-at-the-frame-awards/)
3. AKUART positions the product as a technically performing acoustic solution intended to belong naturally within the interior. [AKUART’s announcement](https://akuart.com/supersonic-wall-panel-gold-at-the-frame-awards/)
4. Steffen Spangmose, AKUART’s Head of Product Design, says the company does not regard acoustics as something added once architecture is complete; he says Supersonic was designed to belong to the space from the beginning. [AKUART’s announcement](https://akuart.com/supersonic-wall-panel-gold-at-the-frame-awards/)
5. Kasper Sanchez, AKUART’s Head of Sustainability, describes the product’s circularity position as being built from decisions around materials, longevity and circularity. [AKUART’s announcement](https://akuart.com/supersonic-wall-panel-gold-at-the-frame-awards/)
