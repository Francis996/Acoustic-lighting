## Editorial guardrails

- This is a trade-body guidance and training announcement, not a product launch, accredited test report, building regulation, project case study or independent post-occupancy study. Attribute the scope and intended outcomes to FIS.
- Do **not** say the programme certifies a product or project, guarantees compliance, replaces a project’s fire/acoustic design, or proves room-level absorption, reverberation, speech privacy, lighting, IAQ or energy outcomes.

## Suggested factual outline for the entry

FIS published a new e-learning resource on October 16, 2026 to accompany its updated *Specifiers’ Guide for Ceilings and Acoustic Absorbers*. The UK interiors-sector body says the material is intended to help project teams select and specify suspended ceilings and acoustic absorbers around both internal-space performance needs and the intended visual effect.

According to FIS, the resource was developed by its Ceilings and Absorbers Working Group, whose membership spans manufacturers, suppliers and contractors. The structured guidance covers good-specification practice and key considerations around fire, acoustics, VOCs, light reflectance, impact resistance, air permeability, wind loading, sustainability and conformity marking. FIS says it contains 36 parameters for creating a safe, compliant and complete specification.

For FLOSEEK buyers, the useful message is coordination: acoustic treatment, ceiling layout and lighting should be specified with the actual room, interfaces and installation conditions in view. Obtain configuration-specific acoustic evidence and lighting data, agree design/accountability boundaries, and validate the finished ceiling against the project’s applicable acoustic, visual, safety, service-access and maintenance requirements.
