## Confirmed facts for the news entry

1. PROLICHT’s September product update introduces ILUNA as a new decorative suspended lighting solution in the PROLICHT ACOUSTIC family.
2. The source says ILUNA uses precisely arranged acoustic slats, which shape the product’s appearance and conceal the light source from direct lateral view.
3. PROLICHT identifies two versions—ILUNA CONE and ILUNA ROUND—and says both are available in 400, 600 and 800 mm sizes.
4. The support plate and acoustic slats can be specified in the full PROLICHT ACOUSTIC colour range. The listed light-source choices are BILO, INVADER or SIGN from the PROLICHT portfolio, plus an E27-socket option.
5. The same item also announces other non-acoustic additions and refinements. The FLOSEEK story should keep its scope on ILUNA rather than imply that every announcement in the product update is an acoustic-lighting launch.

## Editorial guardrails

- This is a **manufacturer product announcement**, not an independent acoustic or photometric test, a certification, a completed-project case study, or evidence of whole-room performance.
- Do not state an absorption class/NRC/alpha value, reverberation result, glare rating, lumen output, IP or fire rating, control compatibility, electrical load, price, availability region, lead time, sustainability metric, warranty or installed-project outcome: none of these are supplied by this announcement.
- The source says the slats conceal the light source from direct lateral view. Do not turn that design statement into a quantified glare or visual-comfort claim.
- Frame ILUNA for B2B buyers as a product whose acoustic, photometric, mounting, service-clearance, maintenance and compliance documentation must be confirmed for the chosen configuration and actual room.
- Acoustic lighting can contribute to a coordinated ceiling strategy; do not claim that the product alone supplies a whole-room acoustic solution or inter-room sound isolation.
